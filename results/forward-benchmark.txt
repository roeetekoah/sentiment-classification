Method note:
The script uses synthetic, already-preprocessed inputs; model.eval();
torch.no_grad(); five warm-up calls; 50 timed calls; and CUDA synchronization
immediately before and after timing. The fine-tuned row benchmarks the submitted
architecture loaded from the cached pretrained backbone plus its linear head;
no model is trained or evaluated for sentiment by this script.
